Disconnect the application

An authorized J&J Roofing administrator can disconnect J&J Roofing Reconciliation from QuickBooks Online in the company's connected-app settings.

After disconnection, QuickBooks OAuth tokens are removed from the macOS Keychain used by J&J Roofing's Jarvis workflow. AccuLynx and Google Workspace connections are managed separately and are not disconnected by this QuickBooks action. Business records already retained for tax, legal, lender, job-history, or bookkeeping purposes remain subject to J&J Roofing's normal retention requirements.
