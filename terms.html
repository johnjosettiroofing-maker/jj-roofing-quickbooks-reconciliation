Terms of Use

Effective September 25, 2026

J&J Roofing Reconciliation is a private internal application for authorized J&J Roofing personnel. Jarvis may use QuickBooks Online records together with J&J-owned AccuLynx job information and private Google Workspace review data. It assists with bookkeeping and operational review and does not replace review by the company's owner, accountant, tax professional, lender, or legal counsel.

Authorized use

Users must have permission to access the connected J&J Roofing QuickBooks Online company and any related AccuLynx or Google Workspace records. Access may not be transferred, exposed to another business, or used for a non-J&J account.

Accounting decisions

Proposed matches, categories, invoice changes, customer or job links, and reconciliation decisions must be reviewed before they are applied. Unsupported transactions remain on hold until source documentation is available. AccuLynx and QuickBooks identifiers are verified separately to reduce duplicate contacts, jobs, customers, estimates, and invoices.

Automation controls

Jarvis may read records, create review queues, and prepare or execute specifically authorized business-record changes. The workflow keeps audit receipts and stops when required facts, source documents, permissions, or human approval are missing.

Money movement

The application does not process payments, transfers, refunds, payroll, bill payments, deposits, or other movement of funds.

Availability

The application is provided for internal operational use and may be changed or disabled by J&J Roofing at any time.
