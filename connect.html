Connect J&J Roofing Reconciliation

This private application is connected or reconnected by an authorized J&J Roofing administrator from the Jarvis desktop workflow.

The administrator signs in directly with Intuit and selects the J&J Roofing QuickBooks Online company. The application requests QuickBooks Accounting access only. It does not request QuickBooks Payments access.

If the connection has expired or was revoked, open Jarvis on the authorized J&J Roofing computer and run the QuickBooks reconnect action. No QuickBooks credentials are entered on this public page.
