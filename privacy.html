Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

J&J Roofing Reconciliation is an internal business application operated by J&J Roofing in Colorado Springs, Colorado.

Information used

The application may read QuickBooks Online company information, chart-of-accounts data, customers, vendors, invoices, estimates, and accounting transaction records needed to prepare and verify reconciliations. It may also store review decisions and reconciliation notes in a private J&J Roofing Google Sheet.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used only for J&J Roofing's bookkeeping, reconciliation, record review, and financial reporting. J&J Roofing does not sell this data or share it for advertising. Data is disclosed only to service providers needed to run QuickBooks Online, Google Workspace, GitHub Pages, and J&J Roofing's secured local automation.

Security and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in a restricted local secrets directory and are not published in source code or logs. Accounting data is retained only for J&J Roofing's business, legal, tax, and reconciliation needs. Access can be revoked from QuickBooks Online's connected-app settings.

Payments

The application does not request the QuickBooks Payments scope and does not initiate money movement.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests may be sent to johnjosettiroofing@gmail.com.
