Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

J&J Roofing Reconciliation is an internal business application operated by J&J Roofing in Colorado Springs, Colorado. It is used through J&J's Jarvis workflow and may compare QuickBooks records with J&J-owned AccuLynx job records and private Google Workspace review data.

Information used

The application may read QuickBooks Online company information, chart-of-accounts data, customers, vendors, invoices, estimates, and accounting transaction records needed to prepare and verify reconciliations. Jarvis may compare those records with J&J-owned AccuLynx contacts, jobs, work types, milestones, notes, and document references. It may store review decisions, source links, reconciliation status, and audit notes in private J&J Roofing Google Sheets and Drive folders.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used only for J&J Roofing's bookkeeping, job-to-ledger reconciliation, document review, collections review, financial reporting, and lender-readiness work. J&J Roofing does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train a general-purpose AI model. Data is disclosed only to service providers needed to run QuickBooks Online, AccuLynx, Google Workspace, and J&J Roofing's secured Jarvis automation. GitHub Pages hosts these public policy pages and does not receive QuickBooks or AccuLynx business records.

Security and retention

OAuth client secrets and tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain, separate from source code and logs. QuickBooks and AccuLynx credentials remain separate. Diagnostic logs contain operation metadata and Intuit troubleshooting identifiers, not OAuth secrets or full accounting records. Accounting and job data is retained only for J&J Roofing's business, legal, tax, lender, and reconciliation needs. QuickBooks access can be revoked from QuickBooks Online's connected-app settings.

Automation and human review

Jarvis may summarize records, suggest matches or categories, flag discrepancies, and prepare approved business-record changes. A human reviews material accounting decisions. The application does not automatically record payments, move funds, or mark a bank account reconciled.

Payments

The application does not request the QuickBooks Payments scope and does not initiate money movement.
